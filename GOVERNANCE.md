# Governance

TSMM maintainers control canonical semantic changes, graph vocabulary, binding admission, deprecation, and emergency corrections. Changes affecting canonical concepts require documented rationale, compatibility analysis, tests, and review. Bindings may be experimental without changing the core model. Deprecation and supersession must be explicit and traceable. Cross-repository mappings do not transfer TSMM semantic authority.
