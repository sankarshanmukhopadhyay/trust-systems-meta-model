# Semantic Drift and Compatibility

Semantic drift occurs when a dependent artifact continues to claim alignment after the meaning, lifecycle status, authority boundary, or compatibility assumptions of the referenced semantic concept have changed.

TSMM v0.24.0 makes drift detectable through stable concept identifiers, versioned projection contracts, repository-local relationship contracts, compatibility manifests, and CI validation evidence. Deprecated or withdrawn concepts require explicit migration or supersession handling.
